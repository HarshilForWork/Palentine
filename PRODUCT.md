# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Indian families planning group trips and vacations, corporates needing staff transport or executive travel, pilgrimage groups, event organizers, and anyone requiring outstation or pan-India travel. Users are mobile-first, WhatsApp-comfortable, and expect fast booking paths — they evaluate trust and price quickly before committing to an enquiry.

## Product Purpose

Palantine Travels is a transport booking service providing luxury buses, tempo travellers, cars and SUVs for group and individual travel across India. The site's job is to convert a visitor into a phone call or WhatsApp enquiry: show the service range, establish trust, surface the booking path. Success is a completed enquiry form or a tap on the phone number.

## Brand Personality

Bold, modern, efficient. A reliable Indian transport provider that feels current and professionally-run without pretending to be a luxury international brand. Direct communication, confident service claims, zero fluff.

## Anti-references

- Overdesigned international travel aesthetics (Airbnb, luxury-hotel sites, lifestyle-photography-heavy "whitespace" brands) — too aspirational, wrong audience frame
- Cluttered aggregator UI (OYO, Yatra) — too much noise, no brand identity
- Generic "transport company" clip-art aesthetic — low-trust, low-authority

## Design Principles

1. **Utility before beauty** — Every section should help the user take an action or understand the service faster. Information density and clear hierarchy over decorative whitespace.
2. **Familiar and trustworthy** — Use visual patterns that Indian mobile users recognize: bold headings, clear service hierarchy, prominent CTAs, phone and WhatsApp as the primary conversion actions.
3. **Movement earns its place** — Purposeful animation communicates confidence and reliability. Every motion is functional, not decorative. Reduced-motion alternatives are mandatory.
4. **Surface the booking path early** — Don't make users scroll to find contact details or the enquiry form. The path to action should be visible within the first scroll.
5. **One voice** — Consistent typography and color across all sections build trust through visual coherence. Each component speaks the same visual language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: 4.5:1 minimum contrast for body text, keyboard-navigable forms, screen-reader labels on all interactive elements, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives on all animations.
